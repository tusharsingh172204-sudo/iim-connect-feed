# Campus Connect Board

I want to build a noticeboard for IIM Nagpur. Each and every club of my college should be able to give away their notices, timelines, extensions, deadlines in that.

This noticeboard should be a website or application that someone can put on their mobile or visit on the web, common for everyone. Everybody who visits should be able to look at the notices and extensions, and select which notification or deadline is more important for them. There should be a section where those starred/important items pop up when they open the website or app, and a section for the closest deadlines of today or tomorrow in increasing order, along with all the other notices.

Each club should have their way of entering notices, and only a particular club can enter a particular notice for that club.

Keep the urgent deadline feed, starred tracker, club filters, and club-restricted posting.
